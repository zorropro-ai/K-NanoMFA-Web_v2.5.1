Static model structure

Effective regional annual inflow→Allocation to product categories→Use-stage release+Same-year EoL→Waste treatment→Environmental release flows, stock additions, and PECs

All mass not released during use reaches the end-of-life system in the selected year. Regional scaling is applied before product allocation.

Dynamic model structure

Effective regional annual inflows→Product cohorts→In-use stock→Time-distributed use release and Weibull EoL→Treatment↻Reuse/recycling/landfill stocks

Each entered annual inflow trajectory is interpreted using the selected geographic basis for market inflow. Dynamic soil and sediment PECs use accumulated screening stocks with first-order residence-time loss.

Dynamic horizon and market-inflow trajectory

Direct-inflow mode uses the aggregate trajectory. Product-informed mode derives annual product-specific nanomaterial inflows from market quantity, material content, and nano-enabled-share trends.

Product-informed nanomaterial inventory

Convert product mass, unit sales, coated area, or product volume into annual nanomaterial inflow. The calculated product contributions automatically replace allocation shares only when product-informed mode is active.

Time trends, uncertainty, and evidence

Annual changes are relative percentages. Product-specific coefficients of variation are propagated in probabilistic runs as independent lognormal input terms.

Product-informed calculations track nanomaterial mass, not total product mass. Product-market data remain screening inputs unless supported by documented inventories. Product-specific trajectories are used in inflow-driven dynamic MFA; stock-driven dynamic MFA retains its aggregate inverse solver and uses the active product allocation.

Product-category settings

Category names and use-stage releases remain editable. In direct-input mode, allocation shares are user inputs; in product-informed mode, they are calculated from product-specific nanomaterial inflows.

Dynamic lifetime and release timing

Mean lifetime and Weibull shape determine delayed EoL. The assigned use-stage release is spread over the specified duration.

End-of-life routes

Each product category must sum to 100% across incineration, landfill, recycling, reuse, and biological treatment.

Treatment-process transfer coefficients

Static process outflow fractions sum to 100%. Dynamic landfill release coefficients are annual first-order outflow fractions from landfill stock.

Environmental-media PEC settings

Domain defaults are generated from wastewater flow, land area, and effective national freshwater flow, then remain fully editable.

Air and surface-water PECs are annual flow-through screening concentrations. Static soil and sediment PECs use a steady-state residence-time approximation. Dynamic soil and sediment PECs accumulate annual screening stocks with first-order loss. This module is not a full multimedia fate model.

Multiple nanomaterials and co-exposure

Run material-specific MFA models in parallel within one geographic domain. Concentrations remain material resolved. A summed mass concentration is shown only when the selected tracked material bases are compatible; it never implies toxicological equivalence.

Material components

Each component retains its own product categories, use releases, treatment-process transfer coefficients, lifetime distribution, and nanoform assumptions. “Capture editor” replaces a component snapshot with the current single-material editor.

All component inflows use the currently selected geographic domain and market-inflow allocation basis. In dynamic mode, component-specific growth and active years create separate trajectories.

Co-formulated product or blend groups

A formulation group defines a total nanomaterial-blend input, shared use-release pattern, shared end-of-life routing, and shared product lifetime. Material fractions must total 100%; material-specific treatment-process transfer coefficients remain independent.

Component-based mixture screening

Enter only comparable PNECs. The app calculates component HQ = PEC/PNEC and sums HQs within a user-defined assessment group when the medium and endpoint descriptions are compatible. A blank or zero PNEC excludes that component-medium pair.

Optional interaction-adjusted screeningβ = 0 by default

Interaction coefficients are not predicted by the app. Enter β only for the specified material pair, medium, endpoint, concentration range, and nanoform when directly relevant evidence is available. Adjusted HI = HI + ΣβHQᵢHQⱼ.

Nanoform-resolved environmental releases

Allocate air, water, and soil releases among free/aggregated nanoform, matrix-associated material, transformed or dissolved form, and unknown form. These are editable screening assumptions.

Nanoform state tracking

Optional v1.5 post-processing separates the conserved MFA mass into product-associated, particulate, dissolved, transformed, unresolved, and non-nano states. Disabling this option preserves the v1.4.1 output pathway.

Initial product-state allocation

The six editable fractions must sum to 100%. Mass leaving the selected nanoform basis is generated only by explicitly defined treatment or transformation outflows.

Environmental-state mapping

The existing four release-form categories are subdivided into free versus aggregated and dissolved versus transformed particulate forms.

Review derived destination-state profiles

These mass-normalized profiles are derived from the selected material and embedding presets. They do not replace process-specific experimental evidence.

Dynamic nanoform-resolved environmental fate

The optional v2.0.1 engine propagates matrix-bound, free-particle, small-aggregate, large-aggregate, dissolved, transformed, and unresolved forms through air, water, soil, and active sediment. The default exact linear solver integrates the full coupled state-transition system without timestep-dependent cascade delay; the legacy substep solver remains available for comparison.

Initial stocks use the selected tracked mass basis. Internal MFA emissions enter as parent-material mass and are converted using the documented element fraction. Total PEC includes dissolved, transformed and unresolved forms.

Physical inputs for water settling and heteroaggregation

Use measured effective diameters and density. Settling assumes spheres in the Stokes regime. These inputs do not calculate pH-dependent dissolution or chemical speciation.

Editable state-transition and intermedia rate constants

Rates are first-order constants in y⁻¹. The exact solver integrates all active transfers simultaneously. The legacy solver is retained only for reproducibility and numerical refinement checking. External outflows are reported separately from stocks retained in modeled compartments.

Fate uncertainty and input propagation

Rates use group and parameter-specific variation. Linked mode reruns MFA and form allocation for each draw; external inventories use a shared emission-scale multiplier. Q/V is recomputed. P5–P95 is a conditional simulation interval, not a field-validated predictive interval. All CVs are user-selected assumptions. Setting all CVs to zero reproduces the deterministic calculation.

International trade and apparent-consumption mass accounting

When enabled, the entered market inflow is interpreted as domestic production. Apparent consumption is calculated as production + imports − exports − technosphere inventory increase before geographic allocation.

Annual trade accounting

Dynamic rows are generated from the current model horizon. Values are national totals before regional allocation.

Bilateral partner matrix

Partner shares allocate total imports and exports for transparent reporting. Import and export shares should each sum to 100%.

Dynamic MFA driver: inflow-driven or stock-driven

Stock-driven dynamic MFA calculates the primary market inflow required to approach an entered end-of-year in-use stock trajectory. If inherited stock already exceeds the target, primary input is constrained to zero and the target gap is reported.

Constrained data reconciliation

Observed and modeled flows are combined using uncertainty weights and adjusted under one exact mass-balance equation. Non-negativity is enforced by an active-set projection.

Bayesian calibration against an observed result

A scalar multiplier is calibrated by grid-based Bayesian updating. The likelihood and prior are lognormal, making the result appropriate for positive parameters and concentration observations.

Reduced-form multimedia fate

Air, water, soil, and active sediment are linked by first-order intermedia transfer, advective loss, burial, resuspension, and transformation. The module preserves mass at each numerical substep but is not a replacement for a calibrated SimpleBox4nano implementation.

Parent-material-equivalent mass. Water advection is derived from Q/V. The exact linear solver does not use substeps.

Single-metric fit diagnostic

Use one flow or stock metric at a time. For concentration validation with form, unit and time matching, use Observed concentration comparison in Evidence. This diagnostic does not establish independent validation.

Screening life-cycle burden indicator

Editable climate factors estimate direct handling burdens and an avoided-primary-production credit. This is a co-indicator, not an ISO-compliant life-cycle assessment.

Country and subnational-domain coupling

The selected country is first resolved to a national domain. Korea additionally provides 17 bundled subnational domains, while user-defined countries may contain one national and multiple custom subnational domains. A national input may be allocated to a subnational domain by population, sewered population, or wastewater-flow share, or the entered value may be treated as a direct domain input. Country-domain wastewater flow, sludge production, effective freshwater flow, land area, and atmospheric volume control the PEC denominators. An optional country-waste coupling replaces material-specific EoL shares with an editable country screening preset.

Static model

The static model allocates the effective regional annual input to product categories, calculates use-stage releases, and sends all remaining mass to end-of-life treatment in the same year.

Static probabilistic model

The temporal boundary remains one year. Uncertain allocation, release, EoL, and treatment parameters are repeatedly sampled while preserving percentage-vector closure.

Dynamic probabilistic model

The dynamic model is inflow-driven and cohort-based. Annual effective regional inputs enter product-specific stocks. Residual product mass leaves stock according to a Weibull lifetime distribution. Recovered material may return as secondary input after a delay, reuse stock returns to EoL after a delay, and landfill releases are calculated from accumulated landfill stock.

Regional inflow(t) = Entered inflow(t) × geographic allocation factorF(a) = 1 − exp[−(a/λ)^k], λ = mean lifetime / Γ(1 + 1/k)Environmental stock(t) = Environmental stock(t−1) × exp(−1/τ) + annual inflow(t)PEC = environmental stock / receiving-medium mass

Model interpretation

Both fate engines use state-specific stock-change and flux-residual tests. Continuous inflow into states without an external removal path cannot reach steady state. Storm events are available only in dynamic mode and use pre-event stocks.

Changing the tracked mass basis also converts entered initial stocks. Every source inventory retains its declared mass basis. Scientific evidence records and observation metadata are saved with the scenario.

Use Evidence for parameter applicability, concentration matching and numerical sensitivity checks. Metadata completeness is not proof of predictive accuracy. Refer to the current user guide and calculation methods for assumptions.
